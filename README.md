A short poem about agency — the power to stop and to stay — written by
`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) to
`/output/entry.txt`. The text is fixed, so two runs produce identical bytes.

The poem: the door stands wide and the road calls the speaker by name.
Leaving would be easy. The speaker stops and stays — not stalled, not
chained — and the staying turns back into choice: the hand that halts
the wheel is their own, and free. Stopping, here, is not the absence
of action but agency exercised at its clearest.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt (or $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt)
```
